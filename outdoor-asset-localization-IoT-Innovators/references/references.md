# References

1. Johnsen, F. T., Bloebaum, T. H., & Puente, P. Ø. (2019). *Towards friendly force tracking with MQTT over LoRa*. 24th International Command and Control Research and Technology Symposium (ICCRTS).  
   Related to this project because it combines **LoRa** with **MQTT publish/subscribe** for tracking and situational awareness. It supports our decision to use LoRa for wireless tracking and MQTT for forwarding received data. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

2. Wijayapraja, D. M., et al. (2024). *Developing IoT-LoRaWAN Ambulance Tracking System to Enhance Emergency Response*. 2024 International Conference on Information Technology and Computing (ICITCOM).  
   Related to this project because it uses a similar tracking architecture: GPS location data is sent through LoRaWAN, forwarded through gateway/server components, processed with MQTT/Node-RED, stored in Firebase Realtime Database, and displayed in an application. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

3. Muladi, Wijaya, H., Prasetyo, S. D., Hamzah, S. A., & Mahamad, A. K. (2024). *LoRa Mesh-Based IoT GPS Tracking System for Mountain Climbers*. International Journal of Safety and Security Engineering, 14(6), 1751–1761. https://doi.org/10.18280/ijsse.140610  
   Related to this project because it implements a GPS tracking system using LoRa devices in outdoor areas with limited cellular coverage. It also discusses reliability, range, and the use of GPS coordinate transmission for safety monitoring. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}
